Privacy Policy – Atlas of Why Uploader

Last updated: 2 November 2026

What the app does

Atlas of Why Uploader is used only by the owner of the Atlas of Why YouTube channel to upload that channel's own videos and set their metadata (title, description, tags, thumbnail, captions, scheduled publish time).

Data accessed

With the owner's consent, the app uses the YouTube Data API scopes youtube.upload and youtube.force-ssl. It reads only the basic details of the authorised channel needed to confirm it is uploading to the right channel.

Data storage and sharing

The OAuth token is stored privately and used only to make API calls on behalf of the channel owner. The app does not collect personal data from viewers or other users, does not sell or share any data with third parties, and does not use data for advertising.

Google and YouTube terms

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Stored tokens are deleted when access is revoked or on request.
